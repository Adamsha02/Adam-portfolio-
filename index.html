01 / About

Business experience meets AI.

I learn by building, testing and putting projects in front of real users.

I spent six years in hospitality, progressing from bartender’s assistant to bar manager. Working close to guests, teams, suppliers and daily operations taught me to spot practical problems and understand what makes a service work.

Now I use tools such as Claude, Claude Code and other AI platforms to turn those observations into digital products. I am comfortable moving from an idea and a written brief to a working prototype, publishing it and improving it through feedback.

I am especially interested in the intersection of AI, creative technology and products that people can actually use.
